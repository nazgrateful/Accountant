What gets extracted

Invoices & receipts

Vendor, customer, invoice/PO numbers, issue & due dates, payment terms, tax ID, currency, subtotal, tax rate, tax, discounts, shipping, total and line items — then checks the arithmetic.

Bank statements (PDF)

Every transaction line with date, description, amount and running balance. Debits/credits are inferred from balance movements and the statement is reconciled line by line.

CSV / Excel exports

Columns are auto-detected (date, description, amount or debit/credit, balance) from bank, card and accounting-system exports.

Scanned PDFs without a text layer need OCR before upload. Set LEDGERLENS_DAY_FIRST=1 for DD/MM/YYYY dates.
